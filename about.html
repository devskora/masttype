About MastType Pro

Your minimalist, fast, and modern typing test platform.

Our Mission

MastType Pro was built with a clear goal: to provide programmers, students, and professionals with a clean, distraction-free environment to practice typing, improve Words Per Minute (WPM), and enhance typing accuracy.

Why Choose MastType Pro?

Real-Time Metrics

Instant visual feedback on your typing accuracy, WPM, and error points as you type.

Custom Modes

Test your typing using timed durations (10s, 30s, 60s) or word-count goals.

Lightweight & Fast

Zero clutter, fast loading speeds, and responsive controls for all screen sizes.

Accessibility & Design

We prioritize accessibility. With high-contrast dark and light modes, smooth keyboard navigation, and custom screen-reader support, MastType Pro ensures everyone can enjoy a seamless typing experience.
